## CPython 3.14.8

Rust reimplementations of CPython behavior that moteval's output depends on: text parsing and `repr` (`formats/pytext.rs`, `pyfmt/`), the Unicode tables generated from the interpreter (`formats/pytables.rs`, `pyfmt/printable.rs`), `json` (`benchmarks/pyjson.rs`), `configparser` (`benchmarks/ini.rs`), `argparse` (`cli.rs`), and the `set` iteration order (`metrics/track_map.rs`). Summary of changes: these routines are rewritten in Rust and cover only the behavior moteval uses.
